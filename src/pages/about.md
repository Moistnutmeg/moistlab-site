# About

MoistLab is my home lab: a few machines and containers, a smart home, and a practice range for security work. I'm studying IT with a focus on cybersecurity, and this site holds the write-ups.

MoistRex, the pixel dinosaur in the banner, is the lab's mascot.

Every note has the same four parts: the goal, the steps, the snag, and what to do next time. They are records of what worked and what didn't, with the mistakes left in.

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Moistnutmeg).
