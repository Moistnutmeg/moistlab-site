About

I'm Moistnutmeg, an IT student in Brisbane, Australia, studying cybersecurity and heading towards a career in penetration testing. The long-term goal is OSCP.

MoistLab is where I write down what I learn. Each note says what I set out to do, the steps I took, what went wrong, and what I would change. Writing it up properly is part of the practice: it turns "it works" into "I understand why", and it leaves a record someone else can follow.

The notes are short and honest. When something goes wrong it stays in the note, under Snag, because that's usually the part worth reading.

MoistRex, the pixel dinosaur in the banner, is the mascot.

The code for this site and my other projects is on GitHub.
